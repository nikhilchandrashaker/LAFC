# LAFC, 2018 to 2026: Peak, Plateau and the Quietest Attack

*Season-level analysis from public data. Written Nov 18, 2026, before the 2026 playoffs.*

## Question

LAFC has made the playoffs in eight of its nine seasons. What separates its trophy seasons from the rest, and where does 2026 sit?

## Data

Season records (W-L-D, goals for and against, points, finish) come from the Wikipedia season list, which cites the MLS Fact and Record Book. Records, honors and 2026 milestones come from lafc.com and mlssoccer.com. No event-level (shot or pass) data is used. StatsBomb's LAFC feed is club-licensed and not public. 2026 goals against were not found in the sources reached, so 2026 goal difference is left blank.

## Findings

**1. The trophy seasons were built differently.** In 2019 LAFC won the Shield on offense: 85 goals, +48 goal difference (1.41 per game), 2.12 points per game. In 2022 it won the Shield and MLS Cup with 66 goals but only 38 against, a +28 difference (0.82 per game) and 1.97 points per game. The 2019 team scored 19 more goals and conceded one fewer.

**2. The middle years show how thin the margin is.** The 2023 team reached the MLS Cup final on 54 goals and 52 points (1.52 per game), 15 points below the 2022 champions. The 2021 team missed the playoffs at 45 points with a +2 goal difference.

**3. Scoring has concentrated in one player.** Bouanga was the club's top scorer in each of 2023, 2024 and 2025 (32, 28 and 24 goals across all competitions). He passed Carlos Vela for the club's regular-season goals record on Sept 19, 2026, with 79 goals in 127 appearances (0.62 per appearance) against Vela's 78 in 152 (0.51). His 2025 total of 24 goals came from 21.92 expected goals, per LAFC.

**4. 2026 is the weakest regular season since 2021.** LAFC finished 12W-11L-11D for 47 points (1.38 per game) and scored 52 goals, the fewest of any full season. Away from home it went 3W-7L-7D. The year started with an MLS-record 540-minute shutout streak across the first six league games, so the drop is an attacking and consistency problem rather than a collapse from the first week. Son Heung-min's 15 assists tied Vela's club record, and the club also reached the Concacaf Champions Cup semifinals before losing to Toluca.

## Limits

These are season totals and milestones, so they cannot separate chance quality from finishing, or team quality from schedule. The 2026 playoffs (first round at St. Louis from Nov 23, as the sixth seed) are not included. Figures should be checked against the linked sources before they go into a publication.
