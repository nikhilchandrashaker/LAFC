# LAFC By The Numbers

A full-history (2018 to 2026) look at Los Angeles FC built from public data.

## Files

- `dashboard.html` — interactive dashboard: points chart, goals for/against, season ledger, searchable records and milestones, Bouanga vs Vela.
- `lafc_seasons.csv` — one row per season (record, goals, points, finish, playoffs, top scorer).
- `lafc_records.csv` — 20 sourced record and milestone stats.
- `paper.md` — short research write-up.

## Sources

Wikipedia "List of Los Angeles FC seasons" (cites the MLS Fact and Record Book); lafc.com (records, season history, 2025 and 2026 match recaps); mlssoccer.com. Data as of Nov 18, 2026.

## Known gaps

- No event-level data. StatsBomb's LAFC data is licensed to the club. If you have access, a natural extension is shot maps, xG by season and passing networks.
- 2026 goals against and goal difference are blank.
- 2026 playoffs start Nov 23 (first round at St. Louis), so that season is incomplete.
- Top-scorer goals are across all competitions, as listed on Wikipedia. Vela's 2019 league total was 34.
- The source count for how many of Bouanga's 2026 goals Son set up differs between articles (9 vs 11), so it is left out.
